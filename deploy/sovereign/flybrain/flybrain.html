The Fruit-Fly Connectome, Firing · a real brain → a behaviour, on nDB

The complete 3,016-neuron larval Drosophila brain (Winding et al., Science 2023) — wired as spiking neurons, stimulated at the senses, watched until the command reaches the muscles.

Pick a stimulus. Its sensory neurons fire, and the spike cascade sweeps left→right through the real wiring — sensory → projection → mushroom-body → descending neurons, the brain's output to the motor centres. When the feeding command (DN-SEZ) wins, the larva feeds; when the body command (DN-VNC) wins, it crawls, and a one-sided odour tips the left/right balance into a turn. Same dynamics as the published whole-brain models (Shiu et al., eLife 2024). Every neuron, synapse and spike is recorded in nDB and time-travelable — scrub to ask "what fired just before it turned?"
