Evolving Ecosystem · an nDB showcase

Many creatures, two species, one world — competing to survive. No fitness function: whoever eats and breeds, wins.

A living 2D world of regrowing plants, herbivores that eat them and flee, and predators that hunt herbivores. Each creature has a neural-net brain (variable topology = intelligence) wiring senses → motion; moving and thinking burn energy, eating restores it, starving kills. Survivors breed with mutated brains — so selection is emergent: populations boom and bust, and brains get smarter where it pays. The whole run is recorded in nDB (creatures, N-ary synapses, lineage, census) and is time-travelable — drag the slider.
