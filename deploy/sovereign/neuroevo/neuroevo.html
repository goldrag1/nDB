Evolving Brains · an nDB showcase

Watch creatures evolve from a 3-synapse reflex into a brain that solves XOR — then time-travel through every generation.

Each creature is a small neural network. Mutations grow its topology (add a neuron, add a synapse, perturb a weight), so a creature's intelligence — its neuron + synapse count — increases as it gets better at the task. Every generation's champion brain is stored in nDB as N-ary facts: a synapse is one hyper_edge linking pre → post with a weight; the champions form a lineage; and because nDB is MVCC, the whole run is a time-travelable history — which is exactly what the slider below reads back.
