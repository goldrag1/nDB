Spiking Neural Network · live activity on nDB

A reservoir of leaky integrate-and-fire neurons — excitatory & inhibitory — wired on a 2D sheet. A periodic stimulus lights the input edge; activity then cascades as travelling waves and bursts.

Each neuron integrates the spikes arriving along its synapses; when its membrane crosses threshold it fires, dumps a pulse down its outgoing synapses (1-tick delay), then goes quiet to recover. The whole run is recorded into nDB — the structure as N-ary facts and the activity as time-stamped spike events — so "what was the network doing at tick T" is a native time-travel read. Hit play, then drag the slider to scrub through time.
